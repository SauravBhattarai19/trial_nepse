How breadth is counted. Each trading day, every security in data/company-wise that traded is an advancer (per_change > 0), a decliner (< 0) or unchanged. The Advance/Decline line is the running total of advancers − decliners. Turning off mutual funds or debentures removes them from all three breadth numbers; the NEPSE index itself is published by NEPSE and does not change.

Mutual funds trade near their NPR 10 face value and debentures near NPR 1,000, so they are classified automatically (see scripts/securities.py). Data: ShareSansar, refreshed by GitHub Actions after the market closes (Sun–Thu, 15:00 NPT). Charts: TradingView Lightweight Charts.
