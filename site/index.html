What the breadth data says

study of 2013 – Nov 2026 · python scripts/signal_study.py
The market deciderNEPSE goes where most stocks go; money flow is the best single guide

The net share of stocks rising vs falling explains about 77% of the index's daily moves (correlation 0.88 in both periods). Looking ahead, the 10-day share of turnover traded in rising stocks (money flow) predicted the following week best: rank correlation 0.18 in 2013–19 and 0.12 since 2020, against 0.16 and 0.03 for the index's own 20-day momentum.

Follow-through happens at the opentoday's move shows up in tomorrow's opening gap, not after it

The correlation of today's move with tomorrow's opening gap is 0.37 since 2020; from the open onward there is nothing left (−0.02). Close-to-close backtests therefore look far better than anything tradeable. Every number on this site is measured from the next open.

Two AD-line crossovers held upAD × its 10-day MA, and the 10-day × 20-day MA

After bullish AD × MA10 crosses NEPSE did 0.6% (2013–19) and 0.7% (2020–26) better over the next week than after bearish ones; for MA10 × MA20 the gap was 1.9% and 3.2% over 20 days. Rules on the 50-day MA worked before 2020 (18.9% a year) and fell apart after (4.8%).

Bearish crosses are the louder warningafter the AD line falls below its 7-day MA, NEPSE rose a week later only 33% of the time

That compares with 48% on a typical day (since 2020). With all five score readings bearish the next week averaged about −0.3% in both periods, versus about +0.9% when all five were bullish.

Breadth cuts risk, not returnsmoney flow above 50% matched buy-and-hold with half the drawdown

Holding NEPSE only while money flow was above 50% returned 15.1% a year in 2013–19 and 13.0% since 2020 (buy-and-hold: 12.2% and 13.7%), invested about half the time, with a worst fall of −19% and −26% against −42% and −43%.

Textbook divergences fail hereNEPSE highs without breadth kept rising: a momentum market

Index highs without AD-line confirmation were followed by +3.0% (2013–19) and +2.6% (2020–26) over 20 days, better than average. Breadth thrusts and washouts were not reliable turning points either.

Slow participation readings faded% above the 50-day MA and 52-week highs − lows stopped predicting after 2020

Both predicted the next week before 2020 (rank correlation 0.13) but not since (0.03). They stay on the chart as context, not in the score.

Mutual funds don't change the signalsunder 0.01 difference

Fund units track the market loosely (same-day correlation 0.55, against 0.88 for stocks). Excluding them gives cleaner numbers, not better forecasts.

What price data can't seeliquidity (rates, NRB margin rules) drives the big cycles

Even the best reading explains only about 1–4% of next week's variation. Treat breadth as a risk filter rather than a prediction.

About this site · not investment advice

Data: ShareSansar, refreshed by GitHub Actions after each trading day (Sun–Thu, NEPSE closes 15:00 NPT). Every share's prices are adjusted for bonus and rights issues. Returns are measured from the next session's open, because NEPSE's opening gap already contains most of the previous day's move. Mutual funds and debentures are classified automatically (scripts/securities.py) and left out of the stock screener.

Breadth: each security that traded is an advancer, decliner or unchanged; the Advance/Decline line is the running total of advancers − decliners. Charts: TradingView Lightweight Charts. Everything here describes past behaviour; none of it is a recommendation to buy or sell.
